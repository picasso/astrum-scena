Light changes faster than the landscape under it. Before setting up a camera, spend a few minutes
watching where the shadows fall and how quickly they move.

## Before the shot

Low sun stretches shadows and warms the colors; a high sun flattens both. Overcast skies soften
contrast and suit close details better than wide views.

## After the shot

Compare the frames you made at different moments. The difference between them is usually the
light, not the composition.
