A coast looks different every few hours. Before a trip, read the tide table and decide whether the
frame needs wet sand, exposed rocks or a full bay.

## Low water

Low water opens reflections and patterns in the sand, but the sea moves far from the horizon line.

## High water

High water brings the waves closer and simplifies the foreground, which suits long exposures.
